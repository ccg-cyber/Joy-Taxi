Before you call

Straight answers.

Are you really open at 4am?

Yes. Nights, weekends and holidays. The number is the same one at four in the morning as it is at four in the afternoon, and it is answered by a person rather than a recording.

How much will my ride cost?

Fares start at $7. The exact price depends on how far you are going and what time it is — a late-night ride costs more than the same ride in the afternoon. Send your pickup and destination on WhatsApp and you get the number in writing before you commit to anything, and it does not change afterwards.

Can I book for tomorrow morning?

Yes, and for an early flight it is the sensible thing to do. Choose Later today in the form above, or simply say the day and time in your message. We confirm the booking back to you.

I do not know how to explain where I am.

You do not have to. Tap My location in the form and your phone attaches a map pin the driver can open and follow. It is the fastest way to be found, especially at night or on a road with no name.

Do you go to the airport?

Yes, around the clock, and an airport run is priced separately from a ride across town. Send your flight time when you book and the car is outside before you need it. For anything departing early, book it the night before — that is the one booking worth not leaving to the morning.

How do I pay?

Cash to the driver at the end of the ride, in dollars or lira. If you need another arrangement, ask when you book and we will tell you straight away whether it works.

Can you take a package instead of a person?

Often, yes — documents, keys, a forgotten bag. Message us with what it is and where it is going and we will tell you whether we can do it and what it costs.
